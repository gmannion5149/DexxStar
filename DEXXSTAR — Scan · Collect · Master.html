      What's computed, what's fetched, and what nobody has

      Market prices are derived, not predicted. Every TCGplayer listing carries a low, mid, high and market price, and the market figure is an average of recent sales. Where it sits inside that range says something real about current demand, and that's all the Market panel reports.

      Sets include the promo runs — McDonald's 2011 through 2022, every Black Star promo series, POP Series 1–9, Trainer Kits, Futsal and the Trainer Galleries. Two gaps worth naming: the 1999 Burger King gold-bordered promos and the Toys "R" Us exclusives were never catalogued as distinct sets by any open database, so they don't appear as their own entries. Bulbapedia is the reference for those.

      1st Edition printings come from the print-run record as well as TCGplayer keys. Card images are Unlimited scans — no public source has variant-specific ones, so 1st Edition and Reverse Holo tiles carry a disclaimer rather than a doctored picture. PSA 10 pricing has no free public API.
